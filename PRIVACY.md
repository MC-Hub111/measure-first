# Privacy

measure-first collects nothing and sends nothing.

- It runs entirely inside Claude Code on your own machine.
- It makes no network requests and has no server, account or analytics.
- It reads the text of the commands, tool output, prompts and answers in your
  session so it can do its checks. That text stays in memory for the session.
- It keeps a list of at most 400 short findings (a rule id, an excerpt of up to
  160 characters, a turn id and a time) in the session's plugin state on your
  machine. Nothing is written anywhere else.
- The author receives no data of any kind from your use of the mod.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/MC-Hub111/measure-first/issues
